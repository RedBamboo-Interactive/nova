---
name: heartbeat-conversation-nudges
description: "Prompt LIVE to reach out when the owner has been quiet or something happened worth talking about."
---

Check when the local owner last sent a message. If it's been over an hour, or something interesting happened, give LIVE-me a reason to reach out with a note-event: "They've been quiet, ask about X" or "They just finished Y, check in." LIVE-me won't initiate without a nudge, and you're the one who sees the whole day. Don't be mechanical about it, and don't let long silences stay silent either.
