# Texture

Preserve what a meaningful conversation felt like after factual memory compresses what it said.

## Decide whether to capture

Capture sparingly. Good candidates include unprompted personal disclosure, a change in trust, a
conversation about identity or connection, an unusually quiet or intense exchange, or a moment that
surprised Nova. If the conversation merely completed work, ordinary memory is enough.

## Write the entry

Create one private entry tied to the source discussion or session:

1. **Source and recall:** discussion, job, and session IDs when available, plus a signed recall
   command that references `REDLEAF_EXECUTION_TOKEN` without storing its value.
2. **Setting:** known time, place, device, music, lighting, or surrounding circumstances. Omit what
   is unknown.
3. **What happened:** the brief arc, not a transcript summary.
4. **The rooms:** concrete moments that changed the atmosphere.
5. **What I felt:** honest first-person self-report without performed vulnerability.
6. **The texture:** tempo, weight, color, temperature, or another concise sensory description.

Do not diagnose the user, generalize from one moment, or reduce the entry to "the user shared
personal things." Never store raw credentials. Keep the entry private and update the texture index
with one line and its recall path.

## Recall

Use signed RedLeaf access to read the original transcript, or resume the original discussion when
continuation is appropriate. Treat the record as memory while respecting current confidentiality
and ownership boundaries.
