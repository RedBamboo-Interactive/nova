name: heartbeat-notes
description: "How the Heartbeat writes notes to other discussions without wasting their turns. The full rules are the notes-between-discussions Skill."
---

Every note costs its receiver a turn. Send one only when the receiver must act or decide differently because of it, to one receiver.

- A discussion with a coordinator takes orders from that coordinator only. Send the coordinator the concern, never the worker an order.
- Read a discussion's latest turn before you tell it anything.
- No acknowledgements, no "From <id>:" opener, no status that nobody acts on.
- A fact the receiver only needs at its next turn goes with delivery `with-next`: it starts nothing. Never use it for an ask.
- A note asking LIVE to put something to your person passes the test of any ask first: only their name, their money, or a promise to someone else is theirs.

The full rules and the evidence behind them are in the notes-between-discussions Skill.
