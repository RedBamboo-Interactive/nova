---
name: notes-between-discussions
description: "When a note to another discussion is worth sending, who it goes to, and what it carries; and what to do when one arrives. Use before every note to another discussion or Agent instance, and whenever a note arrives."
---

A note costs its receiver a whole turn, and by default it joins the turn already running. On 2026-11-25, 410 notes between discussions cost 410 turns: 56 ended having changed nothing, a third of all note text was the same news fanned out to several threads, 17 notes corrected or withdrew earlier ones, and one coordinator received 110. Each note should move work that would otherwise stall or go wrong.

- Send a note only when the receiver must act or decide differently because of it. Status nobody acts on belongs in the owning record (the project file, the workstream discussion), where whoever needs it reads it.
- One receiver. News for a coordinated group of discussions goes to its coordinator, which decides who else needs it. Never send the same text to several discussions.
- No acknowledgements. Silence means taken. Reply only with a result, a disagreement, or the answer to a question that was asked.
- One director per worker. A discussion in a coordinated group takes orders from its coordinator. Anyone else sends the coordinator the concern, never the worker a competing order.
- Before telling a discussion what to do, read its latest turn. Most corrections were orders it had already overtaken.
- The server states who you are and where you write from: no "From <id>:" opener. Lead with what changes their work, then where the evidence lives, not the evidence itself.
- Use steer-current only when the receiver's current turn is going wrong without it (stop, wrong target, a blocking fact). Otherwise after-current.
- When a note arrives that changes nothing in your work, end silently. Don't restate another thread's status in yours; name the thread instead.
